Targeted zip codes

Ad spend is reported by location, so each zip’s share is its share of jobs booked in Q3. Single-quarter samples are small — treat this as a ranking to discuss, and weight the nine-month columns where the two disagree.

What the verdicts mean

Cancellations are kept separate from weak demand on purpose. A zip that books well but cancels often doesn’t need less advertising — it needs better qualification on the phone before a technician is dispatched.

Closing the gap

Three routes to break-even. Each assumes the other two stay where they are, so a combination of smaller movements would get there more comfortably.
